The quick answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize item links and compare information such as categories, sizes, materials and listed prices.

Its value depends on the accuracy of each entry. A saved link or image does not guarantee current stock, product quality or compatibility with a shopping service. This page offers a category directory and comparison guide; shopping links lead to CAFANS.

Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

From discovery to a useful shortlist.

Four checks help turn a long product list into a more informed decision.

01 — DEFINE

Choose one job for the item.

Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a warm layer, a matching set or an accessory. Set a total budget before browsing.

02 — MEASURE

Compare the actual dimensions.

Use foot length for shoes and a well-fitting garment for clothing. Match the listing’s measurement method and units.

03 — EXAMINE

Read past the headline.

Review material information, available photos, variant descriptions and seller terms. Ask about missing details before paying.

04 — CALCULATE

Check the complete cost.

Include applicable delivery charges, service fees, currency conversion and destination taxes when comparing options.

Useful answers

Before you follow a product link.

Understand what a discovery list can tell you and which details require a fresh check.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list that shoppers use to organize item links and compare details such as categories, sizes, materials and listed prices. A spreadsheet entry does not guarantee current availability or compatibility.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and comparison guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS category pages rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

How should I compare product costs?

Compare the item price together with applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion charges and destination taxes. Confirm the final amount before paying.

Can product photos prove brand authenticity?

No. Photos can help compare appearance and visible details, but they cannot establish authenticity. For branded products, verify the seller, provenance and any applicable authorization.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS product discovery guide and does not claim affiliation with Oopbuy or the brands mentioned.
